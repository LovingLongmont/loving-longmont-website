Celebrating Longmont

Loving Longmont is a community-focused resource created to help people discover, connect with and support the people, businesses, events and places that make Longmont feel like home.

📷 A Photo of Longmont This space can eventually feature a photograph that represents the people, places and character of Longmont.
Why Loving Longmont?

A Community Is More Than a Place on a Map

Longmont is made up of the people who live here, the businesses that serve our neighborhoods, the parks and places we enjoy, the events that bring us together and the stories we create along the way.

Loving Longmont was created to bring more of that information together in one welcoming place. Instead of simply being another directory, the goal is to create a useful community resource where residents, visitors, businesses and organizations can discover more of Longmont and help others do the same.
