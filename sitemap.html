Why We Have a Site Map

As Loving Longmont grows, every useful page doesn't need to be placed in the main navigation bar. That would quickly make the website crowded and difficult to use.

Instead, the main navigation can stay focused on the most important sections while this page becomes the complete directory. Whenever a new Loving Longmont page is created, it can be added here.

Tip: You can eventually put a simple Explore All Pages link in the footer of every page. That gives visitors access to everything without making the top menu too large.
