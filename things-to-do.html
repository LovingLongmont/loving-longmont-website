Places Worth Exploring

Start with these Longmont favorites. Always check the destination's website before leaving home for current hours, weather conditions, seasonal closures and pricing.

FREE
Parks • Trails • Fishing

Golden Ponds Nature Area

One of Longmont's best places for an easy walk with beautiful Front Range views. Golden Ponds includes four ponds, 2.6 miles of trails, picnic shelters, wildlife viewing and an accessible fishing pier.

📍 Address: 2651 3rd Avenue, Longmont, CO 80503
☎ Parks: 303-651-8416
💵 Cost: Free
🐕 Good to Know: Leashed dogs are welcome.
Official Website Directions
FREE
Lake • Walking • Paddling

McIntosh Lake Nature Area

A local favorite for some of the best Longs Peak views in town. Walk or bike the approximately 3.5-mile loop, fish, picnic, watch wildlife or bring a kayak or paddleboard.

📍 Main Access: 1929 Harvard Street, Longmont, CO 80503
☎ Parks: 303-651-8416
💵 Cost: Free
🛶 Activities: Walking, fishing, kayaking and paddleboarding. Swimming is not permitted.
Official Website Directions
FREE
Creek • Tubing • Bikes • Playground

Dickens Farm Nature Area

A warm-weather Longmont favorite along St. Vrain Creek. Bring your own tube for the float course, let kids explore the nature-play area, practice on the beginner bike skills trail or connect with the St. Vrain Greenway.

📍 Location: Boston Avenue between Main and Martin Streets, Longmont, CO 80501
☎ Parks: 303-651-8416
💵 Cost: Free — bring your own tube or equipment.
🌊 Seasonal: Creek conditions vary. Enter the water at your own risk.
Official Website Directions
DAY-USE FEE
Reservoir • Beach • Paddleboarding • Dogs

Union Reservoir Nature Area

Spend a day on the water without leaving Longmont. Union Reservoir offers fishing, kayaking, paddleboarding, wakeless boating, picnicking, a dog beach and seasonal beach access.

📍 Address: 461 County Road 26, Longmont, CO 80504
☎ Phone: 303-772-1265
💵 2026 Vehicle Fee: $10 weekdays; $15 weekends/holidays during the summer peak period.
🚶 Walk/Bike Entry: $2 per person.
Official Website Directions
FREE
History • Nature • Wildlife

Sandstone Ranch Visitors & Learning Center

Explore an 1860 homestead, hands-on history and nature exhibits, historic buildings, sandstone bluffs and a scenic nature trail. Watch for deer, wild turkeys, eagles and other wildlife.

📍 Address: 3001 Sandstone Drive, Longmont, CO 80504
☎ Phone: 303-774-4692
💵 Admission: Free during public open hours. Donations welcome.
🕐 Note: The historic house has limited seasonal hours, so check before visiting.
Official Website Directions
FREE
Farm • Animals • History

Agricultural Heritage Center

Step into Boulder County farm life from the early 1900s. Explore a historic farmhouse, barns, farm equipment, gardens and interactive displays, and see farm animals during the warmer months.

📍 Address: 8348 Ute Highway, Longmont, CO 80503
☎ Parks & Open Space: 303-776-8688
💵 Admission: Free
🐑 Highlights: Animals, farmhouse, barns, heirloom garden and special demonstrations.
Official Website Directions
$ / FREE DAYS
Museum • Art • History • Programs

Longmont Museum

Discover Longmont's history, art, culture and special programs. The Museum also hosts films, performances, talks, classes and family activities in its auditorium and courtyard.

📍 Address: 400 Quail Road, Longmont, CO 80501
☎ Phone: 303-651-8374
💵 Regular Exhibit Admission: $8 adults / $5 students & seniors / age 3 and younger free.
⚠️ September 2026: Galleries are temporarily closed for expansion. Grand reopening is October 17, 2026.
Museum Website Directions
PAID
Family • Farm • Animals

Sunflower Farm

A truly different family outing. Meet goats, sheep, llamas, cows, horses and chickens, play among hay bales, tractors, swings and playhouses, and enjoy a relaxed day surrounded by farm life and nature.

📍 Address: 11150 Prospect Road, Longmont, CO 80504
☎ Phone: 303-774-8001
💵 Farmfest: Current 2026 listings are generally around $18.
🎟️ Important: Reservations are required for Farmfest.
Farm Website Directions
PAID
Indoor • Climbing • Fitness

Longmont Climbing Collective

Longmont's large indoor climbing gym features approximately 27,000 square feet of climbing, walls over 60 feet high, auto-belays, bouldering, an outdoor wall, fitness, yoga and youth programs.

📍 Address: 155 Pinnacle Street, Longmont, CO 80504
☎ Phone: 720-340-3640
💵 Cost: Day passes, memberships and equipment rentals available.
🧗 Rentals: Equipment packages are available if you do not have gear.
Official Website Directions
DAY PASS
Swimming • Climbing • Indoor Recreation

Longmont Recreation Center

A great rainy-day or winter option with indoor pools, waterslides, lazy river, climbing wall, basketball, fitness equipment, running track, sauna and more. No membership is required.

📍 Address: 310 Quail Road, Longmont, CO 80501
☎ Phone: 303-774-4800
💵 Cost: Daily admission and recreation passes available.
🏊 Note: Check current pool schedules before visiting.
Official Website Directions
$$$ ADVENTURE
Adventure • Bucket List

Mile-Hi Skydiving Center

See Longmont and the Front Range from an entirely different perspective. Mile-Hi offers tandem skydives for first-timers as well as training and services for experienced skydivers.

📍 Address: 229 Airport Road, Hangar 34G, Longmont, CO 80503
☎ Phone: 303-702-9911
💵 Cost: Paid experience; pricing depends on jump/package.
🪂 Best For: Thrill seekers and bucket-list adventures.
Official Website Directions
FREE PARK / PAID SKATING
Park • Events • Seasonal Ice Skating

Roosevelt Park & Ice Pavilion

Roosevelt Park is one of the gathering places of central Longmont, with green space, community events, the rose garden and seasonal activities. In winter, the pavilion becomes Longmont's outdoor ice rink.

📍 Park: 700 Longs Peak Avenue, Longmont, CO 80501
⛸ Ice Pavilion: 725 8th Avenue
☎ Ice Pavilion: 303-774-4777
❄️ Seasonal: Ice skating generally operates during winter, weather permitting.

Pick Your Kind of Day

Longmont for Every Mood

Not sure where to start? Here are a few easy ways to plan your day.

💚

A Completely Free Day

Walk around McIntosh Lake, pack a picnic for Golden Ponds, visit the Agricultural Heritage Center or explore Sandstone Ranch.

👨‍👩‍👧

A Family Day

Play along the creek at Dickens Farm, meet animals at Sunflower Farm, explore farm history or spend an afternoon swimming at the Recreation Center.

❤️

A Longmont Date

Walk Golden Ponds at sunset, paddle McIntosh Lake, visit the Museum, ice skate in winter or try climbing together.

☀️

A Colorado Day

Start with a mountain-view walk, spend the afternoon on Union Reservoir, then explore downtown Longmont.

🌧️

A Rainy Day

Head indoors at Longmont Climbing Collective, the Recreation Center or one of the Longmont Museum's scheduled programs.

🪂

A Bucket-List Day

Take the leap with Mile-Hi Skydiving or try a new activity such as climbing, paddleboarding or kayaking.

Before You Go

  • Hours, admission prices and seasonal conditions can change. Use the official website links above before making a special trip.
  • Longmont nature areas generally operate from approximately one hour before sunrise until one hour after sunset unless otherwise posted.
  • A Colorado fishing license may be required when fishing in Longmont lakes and ponds.
  • Water levels and creek conditions can change quickly. Follow current safety notices when tubing, paddling or boating.
  • Many Longmont parks and nature areas are free, making it easy to explore the city without spending much at all.
