September in Longmont

Art, creativity and Oktoberfest help welcome fall to Longmont.

Art • Music • Community

ArtWalk on Main

📅
Date: Saturday, September 12, 2026
🕓
Time: 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM
📍
Location: Main Street between 3rd Avenue and Longs Peak Avenue
💵
Cost: Check official event page

Downtown Main Street closes to traffic for an evening celebrating art, culture and community. Enjoy an art market, live music on multiple stages, performers, food trucks and family-friendly activities. ArtWalk is presented by Firehouse Art Center.

Before you go: Check the official Downtown Longmont page for current schedules, performers, parking information and event updates.

Arts • Culture • Downtown

Longmont Arts Week

📅
Dates: September 12–19, 2026
🕓
Time: Events throughout the week
📍
Location: Longmont Creative District and participating locations
💵
Cost: FREE

A week-long showcase celebrating Longmont's creative community. Featured events include ArtWalk on Main, TasteAbout Downtown, Downtown Ink!, Wanderlust Art Fair and dozens of arts, music and creative events happening throughout the district.

Before you go: Individual activities within Arts Week may have their own schedules, registrations or costs. Check the official calendar for details.

Festival • Food • Family

Left Hand Oktoberfest

📅
Date: Saturday, September 26, 2026
🕓
Time: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM
📍
Location: The Garden at Left Hand Brewing
1245 Boston Ave., Longmont
💵
Tickets: $5
Kids 12 & under FREE

Left Hand's traditional all-day Oktoberfest features live music, stein holding, brat-eating competitions, a costume contest, children's activities, bounce houses and slides, a 30+ vendor craft market and local food trucks.

Also note: Left Hand currently lists $5 parking in the dirt lot across from the brewery. Check the official site for ticket availability and policies.

Date: Saturday, October 17, 2026

Culture • Family • Festival

Day of the Dead Family Celebration

📅
Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026
🕚
Time: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM
📍
Location: Downtown Longmont
4th Avenue & Kimbark Street
💵
Cost: FREE

Longmont celebrates the 26th anniversary of Colorado's longest-running public Day of the Dead celebration. The outdoor street festival includes traditional live music and dance, face painting, sugar skull decorating, crafts, food trucks, a children's play zone, vendors and the Gigantes Procession.

Before you go: Check the official Longmont Museum and Day of the Dead information for current schedules and updates.

Performance • Culture • Music

Noche de Museo: Celebrating Day of the Dead

📅
Date: Friday, October 30, 2026
🕖
Time: 7:00 PM
📍
Location: Stewart Auditorium, Longmont Museum
400 Quail Rd.
💵
Reserved seating: $23
$20 Museum members

Halloween • Family • Downtown

Halloween Costume Parade

📅
Date: Saturday, October 31, 2026
🕤
Time: 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM
📍
Starting Location: Roosevelt Park
700 Longs Peak Ave., Longmont
💵
Cost: FREE

Dress up and join Longmont's annual costume foot parade. Kids and adults of all ages are welcome. After the parade, head downtown for Trick-or-Treat Street at participating businesses. No registration is required.

Parade reminders: The City says dogs must be leashed, no large animals are allowed and motorized vehicles are not permitted in the parade.
